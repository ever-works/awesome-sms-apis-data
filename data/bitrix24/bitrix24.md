## Overview

Bitrix24 is a cloud-based, all-inclusive workspace for data management, client management, customer relationship management, reports, inventory, time, etc., and supports internal and external collaboration with teams and freelancers.

## SMS Marketing Features

- SMS messaging capabilities integrated within the CRM platform
- Internal and external team collaboration
- Client management and customer relationship management
- Reporting and analytics
- Inventory and time tracking

## Pricing

Commercial paid platform with various subscription plans. Not open source.

## Deployment

Cloud-based SaaS deployment.
