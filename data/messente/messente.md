## Overview

Messente is a global messaging API provider that supports SMS, MMS, and WhatsApp messaging across 197 countries.

## Key Features

- Message templates for transactional updates, promotional messages, and alerts
- Schedule messages for future delivery based on specific triggers or events
- GDPR compliance
- Global coverage in 197 countries

## Pricing

- Starts at 0.01 EUR (approx. 0.011 USD) per message
- Custom pricing available by contacting their sales team

## Pros

- Easy to integrate
- Solid customer support
- User-friendly interface

## Cons

- Expensive for bulk messages
- Possible delays in sending SMS due to network partner reliability

- G2: 5 stars (2 reviews)

## Suitable For

Companies seeking a reliable global messaging solution with strong customer support.
