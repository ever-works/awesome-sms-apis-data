## Overview

Fast2SMS is a bulk SMS platform focused on the Indian market, serving startups, SMBs, mid-market, and enterprises. It enables businesses to send bulk SMS with quick delivery and high open rates.

## Features

- Language Support
- Custom Sender Id
- User Interface
- Personalized Messaging
- Group Messaging
- Campaign Management
- Customer Support
- Bulk Sms

## Deployment

Cloud-based software.
