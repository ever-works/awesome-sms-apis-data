## Overview

Klaviyo is a customer data and marketing platform that enables B2C brands to engage customers across email, SMS, web interactions, and reviews. It transforms customer data into targeted campaigns and is trusted by over 151,000 businesses.

## Features

- Dynamic Content
- Email Campaigns
- SMS Marketing
- Personalization
- Reporting And Analytics
- Signup Forms
- Segmentation
- Automation
