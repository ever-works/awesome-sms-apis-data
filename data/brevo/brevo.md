## Overview

Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) is a marketing platform that combines SMS and email marketing with built-in CRM operations. All actions are GDPR compliant, and the platform can create targeted pages for effortless marketing.

## Features

- Dynamic Content
- Deliverability Optimization
- Custom Branding
- Signup Forms
- Segmentation
- Time Tracking
- A/B Testing
- Multi User Accounts

## Pricing

- Free: $0 per month
- Starter: $7 per month
- Business: $15 per month

## Deployment

Cloud-based SaaS platform.
