## Overview

RapBooster is a texting marketing software with 800 million users worldwide that provides an easy way to engage with customers through bulk WhatsApp messaging.

## Features

- Customer Support
- Account Management
- Message Tracking
- Contact Management
- Delivery Reports
- Auto Reply
- Message Scheduling
- User Interface
