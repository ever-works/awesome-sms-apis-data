## Overview

Afilnet is an SMS software platform offering messaging services to customers worldwide. In addition to SMS, it helps carry out email campaigns or automate company communication.

## Target Segments

- StartUps
- SMBs
- Mid-Market
- Enterprises

## Key Features

- SMS Polls
- Language Support
- Automated Responses
- Analytics Dashboard
- Opt Out Management
- Shortcodes
- Personalized Messages
- Contact Management
