## Overview

SendPulse is a marketing platform enabling businesses to send emails, SMS, and Facebook messages to customers.

## Features

- International SMS
- Contact Management
- SMS Automation
- SMS Marketing
- SMS Analytics
- Short Links
- Unicode Support
- Opt Out Management

## Pricing

- Free: $0/month
- Standard: $8/month
- Pro: $10/month

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS)
