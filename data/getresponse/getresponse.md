## Overview

GetResponse is an email marketing and marketing automation platform that includes SMS marketing capabilities, designed for businesses of any size.

## Features

- Drag-and-drop landing pages
- Email templates
- Marketing automation workflows
- SMS marketing
- Reliable deliverability
- User satisfaction scoring based on ease of use, value for money, and customer support

## Pricing

Paid service. Pricing tiers mentioned include Max ($165/User/Per Month) and Enterprise ($799/User/Per Month). A free trial is available.

## User Sentiment

Users praise GetResponse for ease of use, intuitive interface, reliable deliverability, drag-and-drop landing pages, templates, automation, and reasonable pricing. Recurring negatives include occasional reporting/dashboard accuracy issues and some unspecified problems with the report system.
