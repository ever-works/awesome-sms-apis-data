## Overview

Yournotify provides the tools and resources that help you create email and SMS marketing campaigns and manage all your digital assets in one place. Serving customers across various sectors and industries.

## Target Audience

- Startups
- SMBs
- Mid-Market
- Enterprises

## Features

- Email Scheduling
- Bounce Management
- Spam Testing
- Campaign Tracking
- Reporting And Analytics
- Unsubscribe Management
- Personalization
- Transactional Email
